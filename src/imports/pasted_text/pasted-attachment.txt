# Dubazaarmart.com — Full E-Commerce Application Development Prompt

Build a complete, modern, production-ready e-commerce web application called **Dubazaarmart.com**.

## Business concept

Dubazaarmart.com is an online electronics marketplace that sells electronic devices sourced from Dubai at affordable prices and delivers them to customers in Rwanda.

The platform should allow customers to:

* Browse electronics
* Search and filter products
* View detailed product information
* Add products to cart
* Create an account and log in
* Place orders
* Choose delivery information
* Pay using Mobile Money
* Track their orders
* View their order history

The platform must also have a powerful administrator dashboard where the business owner can manage products, customers, orders, payments, inventory and deliveries.

## Payment architecture

Implement the payment system securely.

Flow:

1. Customer adds products to cart.
2. Customer goes to checkout.
3. Backend creates a pending order.
4. Backend calculates the final amount.
5. Backend initiates payment through the selected payment provider.
6. Customer completes authorization through the provider's secure Mobile Money interface.
7. Payment provider sends a server-to-server callback/webhook.
8. Backend verifies the transaction.
9. Backend updates payment status.
10. Backend updates order status.
11. Customer sees payment confirmation.

Possible payment states:

* PENDING
* PROCESSING
* PAID
* FAILED
* CANCELLED
* REFUNDED

Never trust the frontend to declare an order paid.
